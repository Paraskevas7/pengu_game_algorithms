CS Penguins for schools

Teach how computers think with penguins. 39 short, animated lessons on algorithms and computer science basics, for students aged 7 to 15, in English and Greek.

Free to use. Every lesson and every practice, on any device with a web browser. No student accounts, no installation.

Open CS PenguinsFree teacher workbook (PDF)

How to use it

Open CS Penguins in the browser and share the address with your students. Each student picks a topic and a lesson. Questions? Open an issue on the project page on GitHub.

Every lesson has an animated player (step by step, with speed control), a “story in pictures”, a short explanation, how fast it is, where it is used, pseudocode for older students, and a practice quiz or practice levels. Students also get a daily puzzle and a printable certificate with their name.

A 45-minute lesson plan

TimeWhat to do
10 minWatch the lesson together on the projector. Pause and ask: “what happens next?”
15 minDo the hands-on activity from the workbook, with no computers (for example: line up students by height and sort them).
10 minStudents try the practice quiz on their own device.
10 minQuestions from the workbook page, then check the answers together.

Questions teachers ask

Do students need accounts? No. There are no accounts and no log-in.

What about student data? CS Penguins does not collect names or other personal details from students. Their progress (stars, quiz scores, certificate name) stays on their own device. Read the privacy policy.

Does it work on tablets and school computers? Yes, in any modern web browser. It needs an internet connection to open.
