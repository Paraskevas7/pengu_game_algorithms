Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Who we are

CS Penguins is a free educational project made by an independent developer in Greece. To contact us, open an issue on the project page on GitHub.

The short version

  • CS Penguins has no accounts, no log-in, no payments, no ads and no trackers.
  • We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information.
  • Your game progress (stars, quiz scores, daily puzzle streak, the name on your certificate, language and sound choice) is saved only in your own browser, on your own device. Clearing your browser data erases it.

Hosting

The site is a set of static files. The company that hosts the files may keep short-term technical logs, such as an IP address, to keep the service safe and running. We do not use those logs to identify anyone.

Children

The lessons are made for ages 7–15. Because we collect no personal information, children do not need to give us any.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, to object to how we use it, or to complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr). Contact us through the project page on GitHub (see above).

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top.
